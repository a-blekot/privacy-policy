# Memorize Shloka — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Memorize Shloka ("the app") is developed by **Aleksey Blekot**, an individual developer based in Kyiv, Ukraine ("I", "me").
The app is available on Google Play and the App Store.

As an avid Android/iOS user myself, I take privacy very seriously. I know how irritating it is when apps collect your
data without your knowledge, so this page explains plainly what the app does and does not do.

## 1. No account, no personal profile

The app has no sign-up or login. I do not ask for, and do not collect, your name, email address, phone number, contacts,
location, photos or files.

## 2. Data stored only on your device

Your settings and progress (theme, language, selected books and verses, repetition and player settings, reminders, etc.)
are stored **only on your device**. They are not uploaded anywhere and can be erased at any time by clearing the app's
data or uninstalling it.

## 3. Data processed by third-party services

To keep the app working, fix crashes and accept support payments, the app uses the following services. They receive
limited technical data, not your identity.

| Service | Provider | Why | What data |
|---|---|---|---|
| Firebase Analytics | Google | Understand which features are used, to improve the app | App interactions (screens opened, buttons used), app version, device model, OS version, language, approximate country (derived from IP), app instance ID, advertising ID (Android) |
| Firebase Crashlytics | Google | Find and fix crashes | Crash logs and stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, app instance ID |
| Firebase Remote Config | Google | Turn features on/off and update settings without a new release | App instance ID, app version, device language |
| RevenueCat | RevenueCat, Inc. | Process donations (in-app purchases supporting the project) | A random anonymous app user ID, purchase history (product, date, price, store receipt). Payments themselves are handled by Google Play / the App Store; I never see your card or payment details |
| Google Play In-App Review / In-App Update | Google | Let you rate the app and update it without leaving it | Handled by Google Play under Google's own policy |

The app does **not** show ads and does **not** sell or share your data for advertising.

Privacy policies of these providers:

- Google / Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- RevenueCat: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## 4. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app asks for notification permission. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

## 5. Legal basis and retention

Analytics and crash data are processed on the basis of my legitimate interest in keeping the app stable and improving it.
Donation purchase data is processed to complete the purchase. Firebase keeps analytics data for up to 14 months and
crash data for up to 90 days; RevenueCat keeps purchase records as long as needed to meet accounting obligations.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/EEA and UK), you may have the right to access,
correct, delete or object to the processing of data about you. Because the app has no accounts, I usually cannot link
data to a specific person — but write to me and I will help as far as I can.

You can also:

- reset your Android advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device settings;
- clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove everything stored on the device.

## 7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

## 9. Contact

If you find a security vulnerability that I have inadvertently caused, or have any question about your privacy, please
write to me and I will do my best to fix it / help you.
