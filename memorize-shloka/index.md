# Memorize Shloka

**Learn Sanskrit verses by heart — by listening.**

Memorize Shloka uses the traditional way of learning shlokas: you hear a line, repeat it, add the next one, and
gradually put the whole verse together. The app plays the verse for you line by line, as many times as you need.

[Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.a_blekot.memorize_shloka)

## Books

About 360 verses, almost all of them with audio.

**Śrīla Prabhupāda's books**

* Bhagavad-gītā — 50 key verses
* Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam — all 12 cantos, 140+ verses
* Śrī Īśopaniṣad — all 19 mantras
* The Nectar of Instruction — all 11 verses
* The Nectar of Devotion — 23 verses
* Nṛsiṁha-kavaca — 32 verses

**Yoga & Vedas**

* Śānti mantras
* Vedic mantras and Puruṣa-sūkta
* Yoga class chants
* Yoga Sūtras of Patañjali — 19 key sūtras
* Upaniṣads — 28 key verses

**Study playlists**

* New Navadvipa Vedic Academy: Bhakti-śāstrī, Bhakti-vaibhava (two parts) and Bhakti-vedānta verse lists

## Features

* Learn line by line: 1, 2 or 4 lines at a time, or the whole verse
* Choose how many times each part is repeated, and the pause for repeating after the recording
* Translation read aloud after the verse
* Sanskrit transliteration, word-for-word meanings and translation
* Word-by-word translation shown above the Sanskrit
* Switch the translation language right on the verse screen to compare
* Open the purport on Vedabase
* Favorites, completed verses and your own playlists
* Day and night themes
* English, Ukrainian, Russian and Spanish

The app is free, with no ads and no account. You can support the project with an optional donation.
