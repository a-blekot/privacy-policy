# Memorize Shloka — Support

Thank you for using Memorize Shloka!

If something doesn't work, a verse has a mistake, or you have an idea for the app, please write to me. I read every
message and reply personally.

## Contact

* **Email:** [aleksey.blekot@gmail.com](mailto:aleksey.blekot@gmail.com)
* You can also tap **Feedback** in the app's menu to open an email to me.

## Before contacting

To help me fix the issue faster, please include, if you can:

* What happened and what you expected instead.
* The book and verse (for example, ŚB 10.21.18 or BG 2.13), if the problem is with a specific verse.
* The app version (shown at the bottom of the app's menu).
* Your device model and iOS / Android version.

## Common questions

**The translation is not read aloud.**
The translation is spoken by your device's text-to-speech voice. Check that a voice for your language is installed
(iOS: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices; Android: Settings → Text-to-speech output).

**Is the app free?**
Yes, all books and features are free. You can support the project with an optional donation in the app.

**I found a mistake in a verse.**
Please send the book, verse number and what should be corrected. Corrections go into the next update.
